Sokoshamba helps farmers, traders, and consumers access transparent agricultural market prices across Kenya. Make better selling and buying decisions using live market data.

Why Sokoshamba?

📉 End Price Exploitation

Many farmers sell produce without knowing the real market price. Sokoshamba provides transparent pricing from major Kenyan markets so farmers can negotiate better deals.

📍 Regional Market Visibility

Compare produce prices between Nairobi, Kisumu, Mombasa, Eldoret and more. Understand demand patterns and choose where to sell your produce.

📊 Smart Farming Decisions

Our market data helps farmers identify profitable crops, seasonal demand, and market trends before planting.

How Sokoshamba Works

🌽 Farmers Post Produce

Farmers can list available produce, check market demand, and decide where to sell based on current prices.

🛒 Buyers Compare Prices

Traders, restaurants, and mama mboga vendors can compare regional prices and source produce directly from farmers.

📊 Data Driven Insights

Visual charts show supply trends and price fluctuations to help farmers maximize profits.
